Setup & diagnostics

This desk monitors two independent encoded programs. It does not start or stop your OBS broadcast.

Persistent settings and credentials are managed by Home Manager and agenix. Changes require rekeying and deployment before they take effect.

Prepare encrypted credentials with your configuration manager. Define each program’s destinations independently, then deploy both service configurations.

Relay recovery: inspect systemctl --user status instantclone or instantclone-vertical. Restarting a relay interrupts that program’s destinations.

Atlas/Murph contribution health and OBS encoder metrics are not available from the relay. Inspect OBS for source loss, audio sync and dropped frames.
